# Aurelia Grand Hotel — Luxury Coastal Boutique Resort & Spa

A visually immersive, five-star single-page website for **Aurelia Grand Hotel**, an ultra-luxury coastal boutique hotel and spa. Crafted with an elegant deep navy/emerald palette, shimmering gold accents, generous white space, smooth parallax depth, and an embedded glassmorphism booking bar.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Sticky Glass-Morphism Navbar**:
  - Translucent background with gold borders and backdrop blur.
  - Brand monogram emblem (`AG`).
  - Navigation links: Home, Rooms, Amenities, Gallery, Offers, Reviews, Contact, and a "Book Your Stay" gold button.
  - Mobile slide-out drawer menu with smooth backdrop blur.

- **Full-Screen Parallax Hero Section**:
  - High-resolution coastal resort photography with vertical parallax depth.
  - Refined serif typography with gold gradient text.
  - **Embedded Glass-Morphism Booking Bar / Widget**:
    - Check-in Date & Check-out Date (with automatic date restriction logic).
    - Guest count selector (Adults & Children).
    - Room Category dropdown (Deluxe, Mediterranean Suite, Grand Panoramic Suite, Presidential Penthouse).
    - Search button that calculates nights & estimated stay rate, opening a personalized **Booking Confirmation Modal**.

- **"About the Hotel" Section & Highlight Stats**:
  - Narrative on the historic 1922 cliffside estate, private white-sand beach cove, and 24/7 butler service.
  - **4 Key Stats**: `120+` Luxury Suites & Villas, `4` Michelin Stars, `98.9%` Five-Star Satisfaction, `38+` Global Accreditations.

- **Room Types Section (Hover Lift Cards)**:
  - High-res room photography, ocean-facing badges, nightly rates in gold, and detailed spec bars (guests, bed, square footage).
  - Feature checklists (*Diptyque toiletries, Bang & Olufsen sound, private plunge pool*).
  - "Book Now" CTA buttons that pre-select the room in the booking widget and smooth-scroll back to the booking bar.

- **Resort Amenities Grid**:
  - 8 amenities: Cliffside Infinity Pool, Imperial Thermal Spa, Ultra High-Speed Fiber WiFi, 3-Star Michelin Restaurant, Panoramic Fitness Club, Valet & Chauffeur, Private Beach & Cabanas, and 24/7 Clefs d'Or Concierge.
  - Interactive hover state transitioning from sand/white to dark emerald navy with gold icon scaling.

- **Photo Gallery with Lightbox**:
  - 8 curated resort images (infinity pool sunset, presidential salon, terrace breakfast, spa thermal baths, marble soaking tub, Riva yacht charter, culinary art, private cabana).
  - Hover zoom effect with dark glass overlay and full-screen **Interactive Lightbox Pop-up Modal**.

- **Special Offers / Packages Cards**:
  - 3 curated retreats: *The Romantic Riviera Escape*, *Thermal & Holistic Rejuvenation*, and *The Haute Gastronomie Weekend*.

- **Guest Testimonials Slider with Star Ratings**:
  - Verified reviews from *Condé Nast Traveler*, *Forbes Travel Guide*, and European patrons.
  - 5-star gold ratings, quotes, slide transitions, arrow controls, dot indicators, and autoplay.

- **Ambient Animated Newsletter Section**:
  - *The Aurelia Privé Guild* subscription with a pulsating radial gold glow animation in the background.

- **Footer**:
  - Contact info, 24/7 concierge direct lines, Google Map embed placeholder, social links, and floating gold scroll-to-top button.

---

## 🎨 Design System

- **Primary Colors**: Deep Navy Noir (`#071318`), Mediterranean Emerald Navy (`#0a1c24`, `#062024`)
- **Accents**: Gold Shimmer (`#d4af37`), Champagne Gold (`#f5e6b3`), Warm Sand (`#f8f6f0`), Soft Cream (`#fbf9f5`)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Playfair Display`
  - Monogram / Accents: `Cinzel`
  - Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Effects**: Frosted glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`), smooth CSS parallax, AOS scroll reveal

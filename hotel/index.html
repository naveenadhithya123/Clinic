An Architectural Masterpiece

A Century of Uncompromising Hospitality

"At Aurelia Grand, every sunrise brings a canvas of azure waves, and every twilight is celebrated with chilled vintage champagne by the sea."

Constructed in 1922 as a private summer estate for European aristocracy, Aurelia Grand Hotel has been faithfully restored into a 5-star seaside retreat. Nestled against steep volcanic cliffs, the property cascades gracefully toward a private turquoise cove inaccessible to the public.

Here, classical Italian marble corridors, hand-loomed silk drapery, and scented citrus groves harmonize with modern biometric spa rituals and personalized 24-hour butler assistance.

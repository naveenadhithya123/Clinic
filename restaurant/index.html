Our Heritage & Philosophy

A Symphony of Flavors, An Ode to Elegance

"We believe food is not merely sustenance, but the most intimate form of art — a sensory voyage through memory, heritage, and pure terroir."

Established in 2014 in the heart of the historic district, Savoro was born from a singular vision: to orchestrate a dining experience where classical French technique breathes in harmony with contemporary gastronomic innovation.

Every morning, our kitchen brigade selects heirloom produce from sustainable micro-growers, wild seafood flown in within hours from Breton fisheries, and prime dry-aged heritage beef.

Chef Laurent Mercier

Culinary Director & Executive Chef

"True culinary mastery requires absolute reverence for ingredients. When you treat a scallop, a morel, or a cut of wagyu with patience, it reveals stories centuries old."

Trained under culinary luminaries in Paris and Lyon, Chef Laurent brings over 25 years of relentless pursuit of gastronomic perfection. Awarded the prestigious Meilleur Ouvrier de France, he infuses Savoro with innovative textures while maintaining deep French classical roots.

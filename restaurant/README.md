# Savoro — Fine Dining & Haute Cuisine Restaurant Website

A visually rich, appetite-inducing, upscale single-page website for **Savoro**, a premier fine-dining restaurant and lounge. Designed with a luxury dark aesthetic, glowing gold/amber accents, elegant serif typography, and Michelin-caliber gastronomic presentations.

---

## 🍽️ Key Features

- **Transparent-to-Solid Sticky Navbar**:
  - Gold emblem monogram brand logo.
  - Smooth backdrop blur and solid darkening on scroll with gold trim.
  - Navigation links: Home, About, Menu, Chef, Gallery, Reviews, Contact, and a "Reserve a Table" gold button.
  - Mobile slide-out drawer with backdrop blur.

- **Full-Screen Parallax Hero Section**:
  - Deep moody ambient background with smooth vertical parallax scroll movement.
  - Luxurious typography with gold gradients.
  - Highlighting Michelin Guide selection, organic farm pedigree, and private sommelier cellar.
  - Direct call-to-action buttons: "Reserve a Table" & "Explore Menu".

- **About Section & Signature Dish**:
  - Story of Savoro's heritage, sustainable foraging, and wood-fired craftsmanship.
  - Signature dish frame showcasing the **A5 Miyazaki Wagyu** with black winter truffle emulsion and interactive pricing card.

- **Interactive Tabbed Menu with Smooth Transitions**:
  - Categories: **Starters**, **Main Course**, **Desserts**, and **Rare Vintages & Drinks**.
  - Fade/slide reveal animations with staggered transitions on dishes.
  - Each item displays dish title, gold price, culinary notes, and dietary badges (*Chef Selection, GF, Vegetarian, Coravin Reserve*).

- **Executive Chef Spotlight**:
  - Portrait of Executive Chef Laurent Mercier (*3 Michelin Stars, Meilleur Ouvrier de France*).
  - Engaging culinary philosophy quote, biography, and accolade badges.

- **Instagram-Style Photo Gallery with Lightbox**:
  - 8 gourmet culinary and interior photography cards with smooth zoom-on-hover effect and `@savoro_dining` handle overlay.
  - Interactive Lightbox pop-up modal on click with detailed culinary descriptions.

- **Customer Reviews Carousel**:
  - Critic reviews from the Michelin Guide, Gourmet & Traveler, and VIP patrons.
  - 5-star ratings, quote marks, slide tracking, arrow controls, and dot indicators with autoplay.

- **Online Table Reservation Form**:
  - Fields: Full Name, Phone, Email, Date (with past date protection), Dinner Seating Time, Party Size, Salon/Atmosphere Preference, and Dietary Notes.
  - Input validation, loading feedback spinner, and instant confirmation card displaying a unique reservation code (`SAV-XXXXXX`).

- **Footer**:
  - Service hours, location and valet parking details, Google Maps embed placeholder, social media handles, and an Epicurean Guild newsletter subscription bar.
  - Gold floating scroll-to-top button.

---

## 🎨 Design System

- **Background Palette**: Deep luxury noir (`#0b0a09`, `#12100e`, `#181512`)
- **Accent Palette**: Rich Gold (`#d4af37`), Gold Shimmer (`#f5e4ab`), Antique Bronze (`#a27c1f`)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: `Playfair Display` & `Cinzel` (Google Fonts)
  - Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Animations**: AOS (Animate on Scroll) & custom CSS keyframes

# 🚗 VelocityAuto Motors - Luxury & High-Performance Car Dealership

A sleek, high-impact single-page website for **VelocityAuto Motors**, an exotic supercar and certified pre-owned luxury car dealership.

## ✨ Features

- **Dynamic Hero**: High-octane visuals, live vehicle stat counters (450+ stock, 1.9% APR, 5-Year Warranty), and quick search inventory filter.
- **Brand Logo Marquee**: Infinite auto-scrolling marquee showcasing authorized performance brands (Porsche, BMW M, AMG, Audi Sport, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Aston Martin, Tesla Plaid, McLaren, Maserati).
- **Featured Vehicle Inventory**:
  - Filterable tabs: All Vehicles, New 2026, Certified Pre-Owned, Supercars, EV / Hybrids.
  - Rich spec badges (Horsepower, 0-60 mph, Transmission, Drivetrain).
  - Hover zoom and glowing red borders.
- **Vehicle Details & Specs Modal**: High-res gallery popup with detailed engine matrix, performance figures, and equipment list.
- **Why Choose Velocity**: 180-Point inspection, flexible financing, 5-year bumper-to-bumper warranty, and instant AI trade-in equity.
- **Interactive Financing & Auto Loan Calculator**:
  - Live dual-sync range sliders for Vehicle Price, Down Payment, Trade-In, APR, and Loan Term (24 - 84 months).
  - Real-time monthly payment calculation, total interest outlay, and visual amortization breakdown bar.
- **VIP Test Drive Booking Engine**:
  - Client-side validation, model selector, driver license age check, and instant digital VIP Pass Generator (VA-XXXXXX).
- **Showroom Locations & Concierge**: Beverly Hills, Manhattan, and Miami Brickell showroom info.
- **Client Testimonials Carousel**: Enthusiast feedback with star ratings and verified buyer tags.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** (Semantic layout)
- **CSS3** (Custom properties, keyframes, glassmorphism, responsive grid)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Loan amortization formula, dual-sync sliders, modal dialog, carousel, validation)
- **AOS (Animate On Scroll)**
- **FontAwesome 6.5.0** & **Google Fonts** (Montserrat & Outfit)

# IronPulse Fitness — High-Energy Fitness & Gym Studio

A high-energy, bold, motivational single-page website for **IronPulse Fitness**, a premier athletic conditioning and strength training center. Designed with an aggressive dark theme, electric neon orange accents, industrial typography, 3D flip program cards, animated stats counters, and an interactive 7-Day Free Pass registration system.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

- **Bold Sticky Navbar**:
  - Electric neon lightning bolt brand emblem.
  - Quick navigation links: Home, Programs, Trainers, Pricing, Gallery, Testimonials, Contact, and a "Claim Free Pass" neon button.
  - Mobile slide-out drawer menu.

- **High-Intensity Hero Section**:
  - Bold industrial typography (*Teko & Oswald*) with neon text gradient highlights.
  - Pulsating call-to-action button: "Join Now (7-Day Free)" with energetic glow.
  - Dynamic diagonal background shapes and athletic hero imagery with dark contrast overlays.
  - Floating live studio badges (*48+ Master Coaches, 24/7 Biometric Floor Access*).

- **Animated Stats Counter Bar**:
  - Scroll-triggered counting animation using `requestAnimationFrame` + cubic ease:
    - **5,400+** Active Members
    - **48+** Certified Coaches
    - **120+** Weekly Classes
    - **14+** Years of Power

- **Programs Section (Interactive 3D Flip Cards)**:
  - 6 disciplines: **Heavyweight Powerlifting**, **CrossFit Conditioning**, **Championship Boxing**, **HIIT Metabolic Burn**, **Power Yoga & Mobility**, and **Zumba Dance Blast**.
  - Hover over any card to trigger a 3D flip revealing class descriptions, calorie burn metrics, training rig equipment, and direct class booking CTAs.

- **Trainer Profiles Grid**:
  - 4 master coaches (Head Powerlifting, CrossFit Lead, Championship Boxing, Yoga & Mobility).
  - Trainer credentials, certifications, and social media overlay buttons.

- **Membership Pricing Table (Interactive Switch Toggle)**:
  - 3 plans: **Basic Iron** (`$39/mo`), **Pro Pulse (Most Popular)** (`$69/mo`), and **Elite Athlete** (`$119/mo`).
  - Monthly vs. Annual Billing toggle with a 20% discount calculation.
  - Hover scale effect with neon orange glow borders on the featured plan.

- **Arena Image Gallery**:
  - 4 facility zones (Free Weight Arena, HIIT Cardio Deck, Regulation Boxing Ring, Hydro Recovery Spa) with hover slide-up overlays.

- **Athletes Testimonials Slider**:
  - Real member transformation stories and 5-star ratings.
  - Smooth slide transitions, arrow navigation, dot indicators, and autoplay with hover pause.

- **7-Day Free Pass Activation CTA Banner**:
  - Instant online pass registration form (Name, Email, Phone, Target Program).
  - Validation feedback, loading state, and instant **Digital Pass Confirmation Card** with unique ID (`PULSE-XXXXXX`).

- **Footer**:
  - Weekly class schedule overview, gym address, interactive Google Map embed placeholder, 24/7 access info, and floating neon **Scroll-to-Top** button.

---

## 🎨 Design System

- **Background Palette**: Pitch Black (`#08080a`), Charcoal Noir (`#101014`, `#16161c`, `#1f1f28`)
- **Accent Palette**: Electric Neon Orange (`#ff5e14`), Neon Glow (`#ff8533`), Lime Energy (`#10b981`)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: `Teko` & `Oswald`
  - Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Effects**: 3D flip card animations, glowing neon shadows, animated stats counters, AOS scroll reveals

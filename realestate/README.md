# 🏠 Skyline Realty - Luxury Estates & Modern Architecture Brokerage

A modern, trustworthy single-page website for **Skyline Realty**, an international luxury real estate agency and architectural brokerage.

## ✨ Features

- **Hero & Advanced Search Engine**: Overlaid search bar with instant filter options for Location (*Beverly Hills, Manhattan, Miami Beach, Aspen, Malibu*), Property Type, Price Range, and Bedroom count.
- **Scroll-Triggered Animated Stats Bar**: Live animated counters displaying *$2.8B+ Volume Sold, 3,800+ Clients, 24+ Global Markets, and 22 Years of Legacy*.
- **Featured Architectural Properties**:
  - 6 luxury estates (The Glass Horizon Villa, One Central Park Penthouse, Villa Bellissima, Malibu Cove, Red Mountain Alpine Lodge, Tribeca Loft).
  - Hover lift, image zoom, live wishlist favorite toggle, and amenity icons (Beds, Baths, SqFt, Pool/Yacht Dock).
- **Property Details Lightbox Modal**: High-res preview with architectural specifications, full amenity checklist, and direct tour booking integration.
- **Property Categories (Buy, Rent, Sell)**: Smooth animated tab switching showcasing bespoke acquisitions, high-end leasing, and global cinematic property marketing with valuation requests.
- **The Skyline Standard (Why Us)**: 100% Verified Ownership, Top 1% Elite Agents, Matterport 3D/VR Virtual Walkthroughs, and 24/7 Private Concierge.
- **Elite Estate Directors Grid**: Broker profiles with sales volumes, closed estate records, license IDs, and direct contact buttons.
- **Client Testimonials Slider**: Verified client reviews with star ratings and acquired estate tags.
- **Private Tour Booking System**: Form with real-time validation, property pre-selection, date restrictions, loading state, and printable digital VIP Tour Pass (`SKY-XXXXXX`).
- **Luxury Global Footer**: Newsletter subscription, prime markets directory, concierge hotline, and legal disclosures.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** (Semantic structure)
- **CSS3** (Custom properties, keyframe animations, glassmorphism, responsive design)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Search filter engine, animated counters via IntersectionObserver, modal dialog, tabs, carousel, form validation)
- **AOS (Animate On Scroll)**
- **FontAwesome 6.5.0** & **Google Fonts** (*Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Playfair Display*)

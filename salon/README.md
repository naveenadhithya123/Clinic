# The Fade Room — Luxury Salon & Barber Studio

A stylish, modern, and appetite-inducing single-page website for **The Fade Room**, a boutique salon and luxury barber studio. Designed with an industrial-chic aesthetic combining dark matte noir, warm metallic copper accents, crisp typography, interactive before/after transformation sliders, and a touch-friendly appointment booking system.

---

## ✂️ Key Features

- **Sticky Modern Navbar**:
  - Metallic copper scissors emblem logo.
  - Animated copper underlines on navigation links: Home, Services, Stylists, Gallery, Pricing, Book Now, Contact.
  - Mobile slide-out navigation drawer with backdrop blur.

- **Hero Section**:
  - Bold industrial typography (*Syne & Oswald*) with warm metallic copper gradient text.
  - Call-to-action buttons: "Book an Appointment" & "View Services".
  - Floating studio status cards (*Master Artisans, Walk-ins Welcome*).
  - Studio track record: `12+` Years of Mastery, `25K+` Precision Fades, `4.9 ★` Verified Reviews.

- **Services Section (6 Hover-Lift Cards)**:
  - 6 services: **Signature Fade & Haircut**, **Hot Towel Beard Sculpting**, **Hair Coloring & Balayage**, **Deep Pore Detox Facial**, **Bridal & Event Makeup**, and **Scalp Detox & Head Spa**.
  - Duration metadata, price badge, service description, and direct "Book Service" buttons that pre-populate the booking form.

- **Stylists & Barber Team Grid**:
  - 4 artists: Master Colorist, Lead Barber & Razor Artist, Bridal/Editorial Makeup Artist, and Beard/Skin Specialist.
  - High-res artist portraits with direct Instagram profile links and individual "Book with Stylist" buttons.

- **Before & After Transformation Gallery**:
  - Interactive **touch and mouse-draggable split comparison sliders** displaying precision skin fade results and caramel balayage transformations.

- **Pricing Table Section**:
  - 3 tiered packages: *The Classic Fade*, *The Executive Combo (Featured/Most Requested)*, and *VIP Royal Indulgence*.
  - Clear checklists and instant booking CTAs.

- **Online Booking Form**:
  - Comprehensive fields: Full Name, Mobile Phone, Service Selector, Stylist Picker, Preferred Date (with past date protection), Preferred Time Slot, and Style Notes.
  - Client-side validation with red inline error states.
  - Visual loading state followed by an instant **Appointment Confirmation Card** featuring a unique ID (`FADE-XXXXXX`).

- **Client Testimonials Carousel**:
  - Reviews from executive regulars, fashion stylists, and wedding clients.
  - 5-star ratings, quotes, slide transitions, arrow controls, dot indicators, and autoplay with hover pause.

- **Instagram Feed Photo Grid**:
  - 6-photo stylish studio feed with hover zoom and `@thefaderoom` logo overlay.

- **Footer**:
  - Studio hours, location with interactive Google Map embed placeholder, social links, and floating copper **Scroll-to-Top** button.

---

## 🎨 Design System

- **Background Palette**: Dark Noir (`#0a0a0c`), Charcoal Carbon (`#121216`, `#18181d`)
- **Accent Palette**: Metallic Copper (`#c87d55`), Copper Glow (`#ea9b73`), Deep Bronze (`#9e5835`)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: `Syne`
  - Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  - Accents/Tags: `Oswald`
- **Effects**: Custom draggable split sliders, smooth hover-lift card shadows, AOS scroll reveals
